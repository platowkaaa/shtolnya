# WHEAL HENDRA — IN-GAME TEXT

Every word the player reads. Five sheets left at survey stations by
W. H. Pascoe, mine surveyor, March 1897 — and the marks cut into the
rock in the Deep.

---

## NOTE 1 — Drift

```
WHEAL HENDRA — DIAL SURVEY, 30 fm LEVEL
Sheet 2. Sta. 5-8. 14 March 1897.

Ground good. Timber sound, no run. Chained 3 ch. 42 li. from Sta. 5,
bearing N 62 E, no rise.

Lode at Sta. 7 stands 3 ft 4 in. and clean. I have taken a sample and
will take another at the 60. Whatever the returns say, this is not a
poor mine.

Left the lamp burning at the station, as I always do. A dial cannot be
read by candle, and I have no wish to walk this ground twice in the dark.

                                                            - W.H.P.
```

---

## NOTE 2 — Drift

```
Sheet 4. Sta. 12-16. 19 March.

My traverse will not close. I have run it twice. Between the 30 and the
40 the workings measure 214 ft longer than the plan of 1874 allows, and
the error is not in my chain.

A mine does not grow. Either the plan is wrong, or ground has been driven
here that was never entered in the book.

The dial swings 4 degrees at Sta. 15 and will not settle. Iron, I suppose.
There is enough of it about.

Two men were promised me. One came. He will not go below the 40 and would
not tell me why, only that it is not his week for it.

                                                            - W.H.P.
```

---

## NOTE 3 — Flooded Level

```
Sheet 7. 60 fm level, west end. 26 March.

Water to the knee here and rising. The 70 is drowned altogether.

The knocking began at 11 and I timed it. Three blows, forty seconds,
three blows, and so on for one hour and eleven minutes, near enough
exact. It stopped without slowing.

Angove says it is the knockers and is pleased - they knock where the
ground is rich. He rapped his hammer three times against the timber to
answer it, for luck, he said. I told him to leave off. He was spoiling
my count.

It came from below me. There is nothing below me. The 70 is drowned and
the 80 was never driven.

                                                            - W.H.P.
```

---

## NOTE 4 — Flooded Level

```
Sheet 9. 27 March. Not for the office.

I have had it out of Angove at last. Since Michaelmas: four men.
Bennetts, Tregear, the Rowe boy, and one he would not name. All from the
deep ends. All alone. None found.

The Cap'n enters them as "left the mine without notice", and the
Adventurers read that in London and are satisfied.

Tregear took the key to the lower workings when he went. The Cap'n was
three days about the key. He has not once been about Tregear.

I am to report on the lode, and I shall report on the lode. But I go
down to the 70 tomorrow, and if the water is too high I shall find
another way - because I have measured a mine that is larger than it is,
and I will not sign a plan I do not believe.

                                                            - W.H.P.
```

---

## NOTE 5 — The Deep

```
No station. I cannot fix it.

There is a stair cut in the rock below the 70. It turns down, and it is
the work of hands, and of a long time. No one cut it for the company. It
is not on the plan of 1874 because it was here before the plan.

The place at the bottom of it was not driven. It was found.

I have set my lamp on the floor and I have not gone past the light. I
will not write what is beyond the light, because I do not want it
written in my hand.

The men are here. I do not mean our men.

If I do not come up, the sheets are at the stations. Someone is to fetch
them.

                                                            - W.H.P.
```

## MARKS ON THE ROCK — The Deep

Scratched by hand, at the edges of the cavern, never at its centre.

```
1.  Tally marks in fives. Five groups and three. Cut deep, one hand.

2.  W BENNETTS 24 OCT

3.  DO NOT ANSWER IT

4.  GOD SEES THE SURFACE ONLY

5.  W.H.P.        (small, by the foot of the stair, apart from the rest)
```

The other marks — the ones that surface under lamplight — are not written.
They are drawn. Nothing in them can be read, translated or looked up.
They are not a language.
